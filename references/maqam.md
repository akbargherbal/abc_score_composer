# Maqam degrees for the Blueprint

The compiler is 12-tone/MIDI-safe. A maqam is written as scale degrees; the
compiler spells the accidentals against `meta.key`. This table is a practical
starting point, not a theory exam. The **degrees** are what the score uses.

| Maqam | Degrees 1-7 | Common tonic | Character / use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kurd** | `1 b2 b3 4 5 b6 b7` | D, E | dark, close to Phrygian; the sample prompt's mode |
| **Nahawand** | `1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7` | D, C | natural minor; mournful but less "flat-second" |
| **Hijaz** | `1 b2 3 4 5 b6 b7` | D, A | augmented second between 2 and 3; exotic, dramatic |
| **Bayati** | `1 b2 b3 4 5 b6 b7` | D, E | 12-tone stand-in for the half-flat second; close to Kurd here |
| **Rast** | `1 2 3 4 5 6 b7` | C, F | major with a flat seventh; dignified |
| **Saba** | `1 b2 b3 b5 5 b6 b7` | D | narrow, aching; the diminished fifth is the color |
| **Huzam** | `1 b2 3 4 5 b6 7` | E | Hijaz-like with a raised seventh |
| **Ajam** | `1 2 3 4 5 6 7` | Bb, F | major; bright |

## Spelling examples on D

For `meta.key = "Dm"` the signature has Bb. The compiler writes:

| Degree | Pitch | ABC |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | D | `D` |
| `b2` | Eb | `_E` |
| `2` | E | `E` |
| `b3` | F | `F` |
| `3` | F# | `^F` |
| `4` | G | `G` |
| `b5` | Ab | `_A` |
| `5` | A | `A` |
| `b6` | Bb | `B` |
| `6` | B | `=B` |
| `b7` | C | `C` |
| `7` | C# | `^C` |

The `=`/`^`/`_` markers are generated by the compiler. Never write them in the
Blueprint.

## Writing the qarar

End phrases on `1`, `4`, or `5` most of the time. A phrase that ends on `b2` or
`b5` sounds suspended; use that deliberately, not by accident. The final note
of the song should normally be `1` (or a chord that contains it).

## Tarab and ornaments

The corpus has no grace notes and no microtones. Represent tarab with:

- two- or three-note melisma groups on a long vowel,
- a turn around a cadence (`(b2:.25 1:.25 b2:.5)`),
- a repeated note at a phrase head,
- a delayed resolution (hold `b2`, then resolve to `1`).

Do not try to encode quarter tones. The 12-tone approximation plus melisma is
the intended SheetSage2 compromise.
